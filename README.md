# Northwind Studio landing page

The contact form for Northwind Studio's landing page. Visitors leave their email address and a short message, and the studio replies within one business day.

## What it does

- Checks the email address before the form is sent.
- Shows a clear error when the address is not valid.
- Sends the email address and the message to the studio's inbox.

## Files

- `src/contact.js`: the email check and the submit handler.
- `test/contact.test.js`: tests for the email check and the submit handler.
